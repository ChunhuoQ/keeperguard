Agents can detect onchain risk, but the dangerous part is what happens next. One bad signal can move funds, and one blind retry can move them twice.

KeeperGuard turns treasury defense into four explicit gates. First, evidence must be fresh, domain-independent, and include an official source. A dramatic six-percent rumor from one unverified feed is rejected. Three independent signals confirm a median price of ninety-seven point one cents.

Second, deterministic policy bounds the asset, deviation threshold, chain, recipient, and amount. The model cannot override those limits.

Third, KeeperGuard persists the exact intent before KeeperHub simulates it. The unchanged body is broadcast once with an intent-derived idempotency key. An ambiguous receipt stops the agent; it never rotates the key and guesses.

Finally, success means proof: completed, verified true, receipt status success, and the same hash confirmed through an independent Base Sepolia RPC.

This is the real KeeperHub-sponsored transaction. KeeperGuard makes the last mile auditable: evidence becomes policy, policy becomes execution, and execution becomes proof.
